Help with BerusAI

BerusAI is in release testing. There is no approved public download yet.

Sign-in help

BerusAI requires Google or verified email sign-in. Email registration sends a verification link; open it, then return and sign in. If Google sign-in is cancelled, try again. Too many attempts may be temporarily limited by Firebase.

A reminder did not appear

  1. Open BerusAI and review any permission banners.
  2. Allow notifications and, where available, precise alarms.
  3. Check the BerusAI notification channel, battery settings, date, time, and time zone.
  4. If the app was force-stopped, reopen it so Android can schedule reminders again.

Android/device controls can delay or prevent delivery. Do not use BerusAI as the sole safeguard for urgent medicine.

Sync or changing phones

After sign-in, reminders continue from local storage while offline and medicine definition changes retry later. A new device restores medicine names, notes, course dates, custom reminder names, and times. Photos, voice notes, and dose history remain on the old device and do not restore.

Account deletion

Use Account > Delete account and reauthenticate. See the account deletion guide if you cannot access the app.

Contact support

Email berusaisupport@gmail.com with app version, Android/device model, approximate time, and sanitized steps. Do not send passwords, verification codes, tokens, medicine details, photos, voice notes, or databases.

Medical questions

For dosage, interactions, side effects, missed-dose decisions, or treatment changes, contact a qualified clinician or pharmacist. For emergencies, contact local emergency services.
