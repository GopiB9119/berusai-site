Account deletion

Delete your BerusAI account and data

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Delete in the Android app

  1. Open BerusAI and select the account icon.
  2. Select Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password or Google account.
  4. Keep BerusAI open until deletion completes.

If deletion is interrupted after it starts, medication screens remain locked. Sign in or reauthenticate with the same account and retry to finish safely.

What is deleted

  • Your Firebase authentication identity.
  • Synced medicine names, notes, course dates, custom reminder names, and times.
  • Any older compatibility dose/image records that may exist for the account.
  • That account's local database, medicine photos, voice notes, and dose history on the device.

What is not deleted automatically

Exported files or copies you shared remain wherever you placed them. Uninstalling the app removes local data but does not by itself delete the online account. Support email records remain under the support/email-provider retention process.

Retention after deletion

Active account data is removed by the confirmed deletion flow. Restricted BerusAI Azure backup copies and security logs are configured to expire within up to 30 days and are not used to restore a deleted account. A minimal Firebase account identifier and deletion-completed status remain to stop deleted credentials recreating data; that barrier contains no medicine content. Provider legal/security records may follow provider obligations.

Cannot access the app?

Email berusaisupport@gmail.com from the address associated with the account. We must verify identity before processing a request. Do not send a password, verification code, token, medicine detail, photo, voice note, or database file. The support owner will provide the approved verification steps and completion timing.
