Questions

Do I need an account?

Yes. Continue with Google or create and verify an email account before medication screens open.

Does it work offline?

After sign-in, local reminders, editing, and dose recording continue offline. Account creation, fresh restore, and deletion need service access.

Will reminders work if I close the app?

BerusAI schedules reminders with Android and restores them after restart. Permissions, battery controls, device settings, or force-stop state can still delay or prevent delivery.

What if I do not respond?

The dose can appear overdue in your local view. BerusAI does not send a repeat or separate overdue notification; you can later record Taken or Not taken.

What syncs?

Medicine names, notes, course dates, custom reminder names, and times sync. Photos, voice notes, and dose history stay only on this device.

When is it on the Play Store?

It is in final testing now. Email us and we will tell you the day it goes live.
