BerusAI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

In one line: BerusAI keeps everything on your phone. It has no accounts, sends no data anywhere, and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Who this policy is from

BerusAI is an Android app that reminds you to take your medicines and lets you record whether you took them. This policy explains exactly what happens to the information you enter. Contact for any privacy question: banothgopikrishna19@gmail.com.

Information you enter

You may enter the following into BerusAI:

  • Medicine names and optional notes
  • Start dates, course length, and reminder times
  • Records of whether a dose was taken, not taken, or snoozed
  • An optional photo of a medicine, if you choose to add one

Where that information is stored

All of it is stored only in BerusAI's private storage area on your device. Other apps on your phone cannot read it. It is never uploaded, transmitted, backed up to a server, or shared with anyone.

Android's automatic cloud backup is switched off for BerusAI, so this information is not copied to your Google account either.

Information BerusAI does not collect

  • No account, sign-in, email address, or password — this version has no accounts
  • No name, phone number, or contacts
  • No GPS or location data of any kind
  • No analytics, usage statistics, crash reporting, or advertising identifiers
  • No advertising and no third-party trackers

Data sharing and selling

BerusAI does not share, sell, rent, or transfer your information to anyone. There is no third party to share it with, because nothing leaves your device.

Internet use

This version of BerusAI does not send or receive any data over the internet during normal use. There is no server component and no cloud sync.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Notifications — to show your medicine reminders.
  • Alarms and reminders — to deliver a reminder at the exact time you chose, even when the app is closed.
  • Run at startup — to restore your reminders after the phone restarts, since Android clears scheduled alarms on reboot.
  • Wake lock, foreground service and network state — used by Android's background scheduler so a reminder is not missed when the phone is idle.

Your phone's app info screen may also list internet, fingerprint, biometric and Google services permissions. These are declared automatically by sign-in libraries bundled inside the app. This version does not use any of them. It makes no network connections, and it never asks for your fingerprint or face.

Reminder times follow the time zone your phone is set to. BerusAI reads that setting only; it does not use GPS or determine your location.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Your information stays on your device until you remove it. You are always in control:

  • Delete a single medicine inside the app to remove it and its records.
  • Clear the app's storage in Android Settings to remove everything at once.
  • Uninstall BerusAI to remove all of its data from your device.

Because nothing is stored on a server, there is no separate account or online data for us to delete on your behalf.

Children

BerusAI is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

This is not medical advice

BerusAI is a reminder and record-keeping tool. It does not give medical advice, diagnose conditions, check prescriptions, or replace a doctor or pharmacist. Always follow the instructions given by your healthcare professional.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of BerusAI adds accounts or cloud sync, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Contact
