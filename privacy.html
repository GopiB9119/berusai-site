Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Summary: BerusAI requires Google or verified email sign-in. Medicine details and schedules sync to your account. Photos, voice notes, and dose history stay on your device. BerusAI has no advertising or analytics.

Who this policy is from

BerusAI is an Android medication-reminder and user-reported record tool. Privacy questions can be sent to berusaisupport@gmail.com.

Account information

An account is required before medication screens open. Firebase Authentication, a Google service, processes your email address, account identifier, verification state, and authentication credentials. If you continue with Google, Google may provide a profile photo that the app displays; the BerusAI API does not store that photo. BerusAI does not receive or store your plaintext password.

Medicine information that syncs

BerusAI keeps a working copy on your device and sends these fields over HTTPS so they can restore on another signed-in device:

  • medicine name and optional notes;
  • course start date, duration, and end date; and
  • custom reminder names, contexts, and local times.

The service stores records in owner-partitioned Microsoft Azure Cosmos DB.

Information that stays on this device

  • medicine photos;
  • voice notes and autoplay choice; and
  • Taken, Not taken, Snooze, and dose history.

These fields remain in private app storage and are not sent by the v1 Android app or included in manual export. They do not restore on a new device and are lost if local app data is removed. Android automatic app backup is disabled.

Offline use and export

A fresh sign-in, verification, password reset, restore, and account deletion need internet service. After sign-in, reminders, editing, and dose recording continue locally while offline; pending medicine-definition changes retry later.

Manual export writes medicine definitions and schedules to a document destination you choose. You control external copies. Media, credentials, and dose history are excluded.

Operations and support

The API may process request time, route/status, request ID, network/security signals, and service diagnostics to operate and protect the service. Application logs are designed not to include email, account ID, tokens, medicine text, media paths, or dose details.

Contact support opens your email app with app/device version and permission status. Nothing is sent until you choose Send. Do not send passwords, verification codes, tokens, medicine details, photos, voice notes, or database files.

Providers and sharing

Google Firebase provides identity. Microsoft Azure hosts the API, private account database, compatibility storage, and operational security. Your email provider handles messages you choose to send. BerusAI does not sell personal information, show advertising, share data with advertisers, use analytics/remote crash reporting, or collect GPS, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

Permissions

  • Internet/network state: authentication and definition sync.
  • Notifications: medicine reminders.
  • Alarms and reminders: schedule near your selected time when Android permits.
  • Run at startup and wake lock: repair reminders after reboot/idle periods.
  • Microphone: requested only to record a device-only voice note.

Camera/gallery are opened only after your action. BerusAI does not request location. Android permissions, battery controls, manufacturer settings, or force-stop state can delay or prevent reminder delivery.

Security

Production traffic must use HTTPS. Cloud records are owner-scoped and protected by verified Firebase tokens. Local data relies on Android private app storage and device file-based encryption; BerusAI does not add a separate SQLite encryption layer. A rooted, compromised, or already-unlocked device may bypass Android protection.

Retention and deletion

Synced definitions remain while your account is active or until medicine/account deletion. Use Account > Delete account and reauthenticate to remove synced data, Firebase identity, and that account's local database/media. Uninstalling removes local data but does not by itself delete the online account or external export files.

BerusAI Azure backup copies and security logs are configured for up to 30 days after deletion and are restricted from normal account use. A minimal Firebase account identifier and deletion-completed status are retained to stop deleted credentials recreating data; that barrier contains no medicine content. Provider legal/security records may follow provider obligations. See the account deletion page if you cannot access the app.

Children and regional rights

BerusAI is not directed at children. A parent or guardian should supervise use where required. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict personal data. Contact support; identity verification may be required.

Not medical advice

BerusAI provides reminders and stores user-reported records. It does not diagnose, treat, validate prescriptions, recommend dosage, provide emergency help, or determine medical adherence. Follow your qualified healthcare professional's instructions.

Changes

This policy will be updated before a release materially changes data, providers, purposes, retention, or user controls. The date above will change.
